What is ABEC Institute of Business and Technology Inc ?

ABEC Institute of Business and Technology, Inc. is a technical-vocational institution in Albay that provides quality skills training and career-focused education. ABEC helps learners develop practical competencies in business and technology through industry-relevant programs designed for future employment and professional growth.

TESDA Scholarship Program

The scholarship aims to provide learners with industry-relevant skills, certification, and better career opportunities.

  • FREE Skills Training Program for qualified applicants.

  • FREE TESDA Assessment and Certification upon successful completion.

  • Opportunity to Gain Industry-Ready Skills for better employment and career opportunities.

Qualifications

  • Open to Filipino citizens who want to acquire technical-vocational skills and improve their employment opportunities.
  • Applicants must meet the minimum age requirement set by TESDA and the chosen training program.
  • Must have the ability to read, write, and communicate effectively to participate in the training.
  • Must be willing to undergo competency-based training and assessment under TESDA.
  • Applicants should submit the required documents for verification and evaluation.
  • Must pass the screening and qualification process conducted by ABEC Institute of Business and Technology, Inc.
  • Priority may be given to qualified individuals who need financial assistance for skills training.
  • Scholars must commit to complete the training program and participate in the required activities.
  • Successful trainees are expected to take the TESDA National Competency Assessment after completing the program.
  • The scholarship aims to provide learners with industry-relevant skills, certification, and better career opportunities.
